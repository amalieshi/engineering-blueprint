## 3. Incident Retrospective (Post-Mortem)

**Meeting:** Post-Mortem — [Incident ID / Description]  
**Date / Time:** [YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM – HH:MM TZ] — within 5 business days of resolution  
**Facilitator:** [Name — ideally not the incident commander]  
**Attendees:** Everyone involved in the incident response + relevant stakeholders  
**Ground rules:** Blameless. The goal is system improvement, not individual accountability.

---

### Objectives

- Establish a shared, accurate timeline of the incident
- Identify the root cause(s)
- Agree on action items that reduce recurrence risk or blast radius

---

### Agenda

| Time | Item | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 – 0:05 | Ground rules and purpose | Facilitator |
| 0:05 – 0:20 | Timeline walkthrough | Incident Commander |
| 0:20 – 0:35 | Root cause analysis — "5 Whys" exercise | All |
| 0:35 – 0:50 | What went well / what didn't | All |
| 0:50 – 1:00 | Action items: specific, owned, time-bound | Facilitator |

---

### Standing Agenda (report drives this)

1. **Current priorities** — What is consuming the most attention this week?
2. **Blockers** — Anything that requires manager action or escalation?
3. **Career and growth** — Progress on any ongoing goals or skill development.
4. **Anything else** — Open floor for topics not covered above.
